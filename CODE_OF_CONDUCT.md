# Code of conduct

This project is for people who keep other people's money straight. Be the kind of colleague you would trust with your books.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Be honest. Say what you tested and what you did not. A clear "I am not sure" is more useful than a confident guess.
- Be patient with beginners and with people whose first language is not English. Plain words help everyone.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks. Do not share anyone's private information, and never post real accounting data, customer names, phone numbers or keys in an issue. Use made-up data.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. If someone is treating you badly, report it to the maintainers privately.
